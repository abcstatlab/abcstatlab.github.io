Statistical tables

Standard normal (z), Student's t, chi-square and F tables, calculated in your browser. Hover any cell to trace its row and column, or look a value up directly.

z, t, কাই-বর্গ (χ²) ও F টেবিল, সরাসরি মান খুঁজে নেওয়ার সুবিধাসহ।

Standard normal table: area to the left of z

Row gives z to one decimal, column adds the second decimal. Example: z = 1.96 is row 1.9, column 0.06, giving 0.9750.

Student's t table: critical values

Each value t has the stated area α to its right. Use the two-tail row for two-sided tests and confidence intervals. The ∞ row equals the z value.

Chi-square table: critical values

Each value has the stated area α to its right. For a goodness-of-fit or independence test, compare your χ² statistic with the value in the α = 0.05 column.

F table: critical values

Columns are the numerator degrees of freedom (df₁), rows the denominator (df₂). Each value has area α to its right. In one-way ANOVA, df₁ = groups − 1 and df₂ = total observations − groups; reject H₀ if your F is larger than the table value.
